COMMON QUESTIONS

A few things
worth knowing.

Good partnerships begin with clear expectations.

Can you host a website I already have?

Possibly. It depends on the platform, domain setup, and features. We assess the site before recommending a transfer, rebuild, or management-in-place. Request an assessment.

Is SEO part of every website?

New builds include a foundational technical SEO setup. Ongoing SEO strategy, local optimization, content, and reporting are scoped separately.

Do I need to switch all my software?

No. We start with your existing tools and only recommend replacements when the benefits justify the change.

How are projects and support priced?

We provide a scope and quote after learning about your needs. Website builds, hosting, care, SEO, and automation are priced according to the work involved.

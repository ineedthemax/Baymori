# Care on client-owned hosting

Offer management in place after compatibility review. Hosting location and care responsibility are separate. Do not force a migration to sell support.

- Client owns and pays their hosting, domain and software subscriptions.
- Baymori receives delegated access appropriate to the agreed work; do not collect passwords through intake.
- Keep care starting prices subject to an assessment and written scope. Do not automatically discount because hosting stays elsewhere.
- Document who handles hosting availability, site updates, licenses, backups, restoration, email/DNS and incident escalation.
- Audit inherited problems and quote cleanup/onboarding separately before activating care. Complex WordPress/plugins, commerce or unsupported custom stacks require their own quote or a decline.
- Monitor what can actually be tested. An HTTP success is not proof that forms or checkout work.
- The onboarding page has a dedicated keep-hosting path. This page is invitation-oriented and noindexed, but not an authenticated client portal.
- Delivery currently uses a browser-generated text download which clients email manually. No server submission, database storage, automated email or dashboard sync is claimed.

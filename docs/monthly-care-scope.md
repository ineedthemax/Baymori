# Baymori Monthly Website Care: Service Scope (Draft)

**Status:** Internal operating draft. Not a signed contract, legal advice, or a promise of services not yet operational. Review operational readiness and agreement language before accepting payments.

## Positioning

Baymori provides managed hosting and defined website maintenance for compatible business and creator websites. Plans are offered following a platform/complexity assessment and an approved written scope. **One website per plan.** Migrations, initial setup, domain registration, external tools, and separate projects are quoted individually.

## Essential Care | starting at $99/month

For one eligible website:
- Hosting on an approved Baymori-managed setup, with SSL and configuration oversight.
- Basic uptime/availability monitoring and routine technical/security/dependency checks.
- Initial troubleshooting for hosting-related issues within the agreed responsibility boundary.
- Email support: target initial response within **2 business days** during designated business hours.

Not included: content edits, redesigns, new pages or integrations, marketing, performance guarantees, or third-party subscription charges. Specify monitoring cadence, incident handling, backups and recovery before sale.

## Business Care | starting at $199/month

Everything in Essential, plus:
- **Up to 60 minutes per billing month** of minor editing on existing website content, using client-supplied copy and assets.
- Monthly website health review, including checking key links and client inquiry forms where accessible.
- Basic performance and technical search-health monitoring, not a managed SEO campaign.
- Email support: target initial response within **1 business day** during designated business hours.

**Minor edits:** changing existing text, image, link, button label, or similar low-complexity content without new layout, code feature, or content creation. Request time covers investigation, changes, testing and coordination. Unused time does not roll over. Additional work needs a separate written quote and approval.

## Custom/Growth

Higher-frequency updates and complex sites are **custom quoted only** after assessment. Do not advertise a standard $349 tier until delivery capacity and pricing are validated.

## Exclusions from all plans unless separately agreed

- New pages, redesigns, new custom code and major feature or functionality requests.
- New AI automations, external-system integrations, complex booking, membership or ecommerce changes.
- Ongoing SEO strategy, keyword research, content publishing, backlink outreach, advertising, or analytics consulting.
- Site migration, email hosting, domain registrations/renewals, paid themes/plugins and other third-party subscriptions.
- Security incident remediation originating outside Baymori's agreed scope, and remediation of unsupported software.
- Writing copy, creating photography/graphics/video, accessibility audits, regulatory compliance guarantees, or unlimited revisions.

## Operating boundaries to approve before contracts

1. **Eligibility:** Confirm hosting platform, site inventory, database/server requirements, traffic, business criticality, and third-party dependencies. Cloudflare static hosting does not support every site.
2. **Ownership and access:** Client retains domain/content/IP ownership. Document who owns hosting accounts, DNS, credentials, third-party subscriptions, and what is transferred on cancellation. Use delegated access and secure secrets, not passwords sent through inquiry forms.
3. **Requests:** Centralized written requests and a queue; monthly editing time metered. Define business hours, response channels, holidays, and scheduling. Initial response is not a resolution SLA.
4. **Hosting and incidents:** Document availability monitoring and escalation, severity definitions, external vendor dependency, acceptable-use and resource limits. Never guarantee 100% uptime.
5. **Backups:** State precisely **whether backups are included**, what is backed up (code, content, database), frequency, retention, who can restore, and whether restores are tested. Do not claim a backup SLA until it exists.
6. **Billing:** Monthly advance billing, start dates, payment method, failed payments, late fees where legally permitted, tax, annual pricing changes, and whether setup fees apply.
7. **Cancellation:** Define notice period, effective date, support/access end date, export/migration assistance, and applicable fees. No hostage tactics over client domains.
8. **Change orders:** Quote and approve work outside plan before beginning. No implied unlimited support.
9. **Protection:** Confidentiality, data handling, liability limits and exclusions, service suspension, IP, content legality and third-party terms to be addressed in a professionally reviewed agreement.
10. **Profitability:** Track monitoring cost, actual hosting usage, support tickets, editing minutes, and gross margin per client each month. Reprice or decline high-maintenance stacks.

## Sales process

1. Inquiry about hosting or monthly care.
2. Compatibility / risk / workload review.
3. Written proposal with exact inclusions, exclusions, site list, hosting topology, and monthly price.
4. Signed care agreement and payment authorization.
5. Onboarding inventory, access delegation, monitoring and (where contracted) backup verification.
6. Service begins after a documented go-live handoff.

**Pricing is a starting point for eligible websites, never a universal promise across all platforms.**
